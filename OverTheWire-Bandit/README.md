# OverTheWire — Bandit

Bandit was the first wargame I actually stuck with. It's all SSH into a box,
hunt for a password hidden somewhere on the filesystem, use it to log into the
next level. Simple loop, but it's the thing that finally made the shell click
for me instead of being something I copied off tutorials.

Screenshots in here are the moment each level popped. I kept them mostly so I
can look back and see how slow I was at the start.

## Levels

`bandit-00-01.png` through `bandit-11-12.png` — one shot per level, named for
the jump it captures. So `bandit-06-07.png` is me getting out of level 6 and
into 7.

The early ones (0 through 2) are from Aug 29. Everything from 2→3 up to 11→12
I did in one sitting the next afternoon, which is why the timestamps are all
a few minutes apart.
